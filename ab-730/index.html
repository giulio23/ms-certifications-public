Common Exam Traps

Distinctions AB-730 loves to test

Copilot chat vs an agent

Chat answers in the moment from your context. An agent is a reusable assistant you configure once — knowledge, instructions, capabilities — and share with a team. Build an agent when the same task repeats for many people.

Copilot differs across M365 apps

Same Copilot, different job per app: Word drafts & rewrites, Excel analyzes & formulas, Teams recaps meetings & catches you up, Outlook summarizes threads & drafts replies. Match the app to the task.

Fabrication & verification

Copilot can produce plausible-but-wrong content. Check the citations, verify against the source, and apply human review before you act — never treat a first draft as fact.

Prompt injection & over-reliance

Untrusted content (a pasted email, a web page) can carry hidden instructions that hijack the prompt. Stay skeptical, keep a human in the loop, and don't over-rely on output for decisions.

Data protection limits results

Copilot only surfaces content you already have permission to see. Sensitivity labels, permissions, and data-protection policies restrict what it returns — it never bypasses access controls.

Anatomy of an effective prompt

A strong prompt gives goal + context + source + expectations. Vague prompts get vague answers. Reference files, people, and resources — then save, schedule, and share the prompts that work.
