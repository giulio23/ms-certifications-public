Common Exam Traps

Distinctions AB-731 loves to test

Generative AI vs classic AI/ML

Generative AI creates new content (text, images, code) from a prompt. Classic ML predicts/classifies from patterns (forecasting, fraud, vision). Pick gen AI for content/summarize/assist; pick ML for prediction on structured data.

Pretrained vs fine-tuned

Start with a pretrained model + good prompts + grounding (RAG). Only fine-tune when you need consistent style/format/domain behavior that prompting and grounding can't achieve — it costs more data, effort, and money.

Grounding / RAG vs fabrication

Grounding gives the model your trusted data at answer time (RAG) so responses cite real sources. Without it, models can fabricate (hallucinate) plausible-but-wrong answers. Grounding is the main lever for reliability.

M365 Copilot vs Azure AI (Foundry)

M365 Copilot = ready-made AI in Word/Excel/Teams/Outlook grounded on Microsoft Graph — buy & adopt. Azure AI / Foundry = build custom solutions with models and services (Vision, AI Search, OpenAI). Buy Copilot vs build on Azure.

Responsible AI principles

Six/seven Microsoft RAI principles: fairness, reliability & safety, privacy & security, inclusiveness, transparency, accountability. Governance + an AI council put them into practice — know which principle a scenario maps to.

Tokens, ROI & adoption

Gen AI cost is driven by tokens (input + output); weigh it against ROI. Adoption is a change-management effort — an adoption team, AI champions, and attention to data/security/privacy/cost and the right Copilot license.
